ABSTRACT :
This professional dissertation reports on the design and development of a web platform 
covering the Festival of Agricultural and Agritech Innovations (FIAA), carried out at  
APTIC-R from 5 December 2025 to 15 May 2026. The work addresses scattered event  
information  and  the  lack  of  a  single  channel  for  registration,  badges  and  access 
control. Needs analysis, UML and MERISE modelling, then iterative development of a 
React, TypeScript and Supabase application, also packaged for Android, produced a  
public site, a programme, profile-based registration, a participant area, an admin back  
office, QR badges and a scanner. Editorial modules (news, gallery) remain future  
work. Priority next steps are account security and a single source of event identity.
Keywords: web  platform;  event  coverage;  agritech;  FIAA;  APTIC-R;  React; 
Supabase.
